Generate one landscape 3:2 cinematic editorial photograph, realistic materials, documentary camera, careful observational details, low-key atmospheric light but clearly readable objects, muted blue shadows and warm practical lighting, no people, no gore, no logos, no overlaid captions, no watermarks, no collage, no UI. Scene: [scene] Preserve all these facts, do not add weapons, evidence markers or supernatural elements.
